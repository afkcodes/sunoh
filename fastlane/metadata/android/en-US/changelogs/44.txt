Lossless streaming. Settings > Stream quality has a new Lossless option.
When a track exists in a lossless catalog, sunoh streams it straight from
that catalog - nothing passes through our servers. A small tag tells you
what you are actually hearing, read from the player itself.

Gaana tracks can now be downloaded. They are served as streaming
playlists rather than files, which is why they were the one source you
could not save.

Your device library, properly: sort by date added, title, artist, album,
duration or year, new Genres and Folders tabs, and search across
everything on the phone. Albums now play in track order. Music you copy
onto the phone shows up on its own.

Playback fixes: audio was stuttering because the player's buffer was
never being set. Playing offline now stops and waits for the connection
instead of racing through the queue. Downloads show progress on the song
row, and your saved position appears as soon as the app opens.

Swiping the app away from recents now actually closes it.
