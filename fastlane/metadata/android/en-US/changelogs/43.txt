Sync your library between two phones - liked songs, playlists, podcasts and
settings - with no account. sunoh writes an encrypted file into a folder you
pick, and whatever already syncs that folder moves it.

Choose which folders on your phone count as music, so ringtones and voice
notes stay out of your library. Pick nothing and you get everything.

Optionally sign in to YouTube Music for your own recommendations and library.
It uses Google's own sign-in page, the app never sees your password, and the
session stays encrypted on your phone. Everything works without it.

The YouTube home feed now shows everything it has rather than a fraction.

A faster home screen, artwork that no longer flickers on the way back from an
album, and download progress on the song row.
