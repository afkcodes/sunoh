Three big additions: your car, your own music files, and a light theme.

Android Auto - browse, search and play without touching the phone. Radio
stations play in one tap; voice search works.

On this device - your own audio files in the Library, by song, album and
artist, with their embedded art.

Light theme - Settings, Appearance, Theme.

No analytics of any kind; the SDK was removed outright.

Fixed: radio stations that did nothing, and missing album art in
notifications for local tracks.
