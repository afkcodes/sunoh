sunoh plays music from YouTube Music, Gaana and Saavn behind a single search,
alongside the music already stored on your phone. Podcasts and audiobooks too.

No ads. No account. No tracking.

Sources

* YouTube Music - search, the home feed with its editorial rows, moods and
  genres, playlists, albums, and artist pages with one-tap radio. It talks to
  the same InnerTube API the official client does, not a web wrapper.
* Gaana and Saavn - trending, charts, playlists and albums, with strong
  Indian-language coverage.
* On this device - your own music files, browsable by song, album and artist,
  with the album art embedded in them.
* Results from every source appear in one search.

Playback

* mpv as the audio engine: gapless queue, shuffle, repeat, reorderable queue.
* SponsorBlock on YouTube tracks, skipping sponsor reads, intros, outros and
  non-music segments. Only a short hash prefix of the video id is sent.
* Equalizer, sleep timer, and Chromecast.
* Synced lyrics via LRCLIB.
* Media notification with a working seekbar, and full background playback.

Android Auto

Full browse and playback in the car, including downloads, liked songs, recently
played, playlists, the music, podcast and audiobook feeds, radio stations, and
voice search.

Library

Liked songs, user playlists, recently played, offline downloads, and playlist
import from Spotify.

Privacy

No sign-in anywhere. Analytics is opt-out and limited to event counts; turning
it off halts collection and resets the local install id. On-device music never
leaves the phone.

sunoh is an independent client, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the
services it reaches, and hosts no media of its own.
